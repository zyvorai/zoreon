# Zoreon: enterprise subscription

Zoreon is available under an annual **enterprise subscription** for production use. One subscription covers product access, updates and upgrades, and the stated support level. There is no separate support surcharge.

| Plan | What you get | Coverage |
|------|--------------|----------|
| Community | Evaluation and non-production use, where the licence allows it | Community resources |
| Professional | Entry edition, updates and upgrades | Business hours, next-business-day critical response |
| Enterprise | Advanced edition, updates and upgrades | Priority business hours, four-business-hour critical response |
| Premium | Contracted enterprise scope and critical escalation | 24x7 critical intake, one-hour initial response target |

Rates: USD 3 per user per month (INR 297 per user per month incl. 18% GST in India), minimum 25 users, an annual minimum of USD 900 (INR 89,208 incl. GST). Non-production use stays free under the Zyvor Production License v1.0.

Prices: INR incl. 18% GST in India; US dollars excl. tax elsewhere (VAT, GST or sales tax added where applicable). Pay online by UPI, card or netbanking where a buy page exists; international cards work (charged in rupees); or request a US-dollar invoice by bank transfer. Custom pricing: contact [sales@zyvor.dev](mailto:sales@zyvor.dev). Buy / get a quote: <https://zyvor.dev/buy>.

## Terms

- Annual prepaid subscription is the default, with a 12-month minimum. Multi-year prepay is available.
- Minimums apply after any discounts.
- Implementation, migration, training and managed operations are separate services.
- At expiry, updates, downloads and support end. Rights under the Zyvor Production License v1.0 continue per its terms.
- Response targets need a contracted scope and are not uptime guarantees.
- Existing signed agreements keep their agreed terms.
